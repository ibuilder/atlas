What SCIM is for

Offboarding. Somebody leaves, HR disables them in the directory, and their access here goes with it without anybody remembering to do anything. A PATCH setting active: false disables the account and revokes its live sessions in the same operation — marking a user inactive while leaving a session token is offboarding that does not offboard. Accounts owned by a directory are read-only here, because a local edit would otherwise either be reverted at the next sync or silently override an offboarding, depending on timing.

The endpoint is /scim/v2. Deletion deactivates rather than removing the row: a user id appears on ledger entries, audit events, and approvals.
